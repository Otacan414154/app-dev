## True Beauty
A 2020 Korean drama on tvN with 16 episodes, based on a webtoon. It is about a girl who is bullied for her looks, learns makeup, and becomes popular.

**Main cast**
- Moon Ga-young as Lim Ju-kyung
- Cha Eun-woo as Lee Su-ho
- Hwang In-youp as Han Seo-jun

**Why I love it**
- It teaches that *real confidence* comes from accepting yourself.
- The mix of **comedy** and **romance** is sweet and funny.

---

## The Prisoner of Beauty
A 2025 Chinese period drama with 36 episodes. Xiao Qiao marries Wei Shao even though their families are rivals, and the two slowly learn to trust each other.

**Main cast**
- Song Zuer as Xiao Qiao
- Liu Yuning as Wei Shao

**Why I love it**
- I enjoy the **enemies-to-lovers** story as the couple grows closer.
- The costumes and the period setting are beautiful.

---

## Pursuit of Jade
A 2026 Chinese period drama with 40 episodes. Fan Changyu, a butcher's daughter, enters a fake marriage with Xie Zheng, a fallen noble, and it turns into real love.

**Main cast**
- Zhang Linghe as Xie Zheng
- Tian Xiwei as Fan Changyu

**Why I love it**
- The *fake marriage turned real love* is my favorite kind of story.
- Fan Changyu is a **strong and brave** lead character.

---

## Firefly Wedding
A 2026 Japanese anime that started airing on October 9, 2026. It is about the sickly daughter of a noble who proposes to the assassin who was hired to kill her.

**Main characters**
- Satoko
- Shinpei

**Why I love it**
- The story is **unusual** and different from other romances.
- The dark and dramatic mood keeps me curious about what happens next.

---

## What's Wrong with Secretary Kim
A 2018 Korean drama on tvN with 16 episodes. A vice chairman who thinks he is perfect panics when his secretary of many years decides to quit.

**Main cast**
- Park Seo-joon as Lee Young-joon
- Park Min-young as Kim Mi-so

**Why I love it**
- It is a *light and funny* office romance.
- The two leads have great chemistry.

---

## When Life Gives You Tangerines
A 2025 Korean drama on Netflix with 16 episodes. It follows a love story and a family across many years in Jeju Island.

**Main cast**
- IU as Oh Ae-sun
- Park Bo-gum as Yang Gwan-sik

**Why I love it**
- It is **emotional** and shows the love between family members.
- The Jeju Island setting is beautiful.
